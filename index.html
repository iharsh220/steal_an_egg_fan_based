FAQ

Quick answers

What should a new player do first?

Open the verified experience, place an egg and hatch your first pet. Then reinvest what your pets earn into the treadmill and base before trying to steal.

How do pets make money?

Hatched pets earn money for you over time. Spend it on base and treadmill upgrades. Exact earning rates aren't published, so check in-game.

How does pet fusion work?

Fusion details aren't verified from official sources yet. Treat community claims as leads, and check the in-game UI and official update notes.

Are there active codes?

No. No verified code string or redemption interface has been found. Be careful with "free codes" sites.

Is there an official Discord?

Only trust community links shown on the game's official Roblox page. If a link isn't there, assume it's unofficial.
